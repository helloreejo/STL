Questions, answered.

The things clients usually ask before we start. Anything else? Ask us directly.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Brand identities, websites, web and mobile apps, custom software and AI features — from the first version of a new idea to improving a product that's already live. If it involves design, technology or growth, we're happy to talk it through.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we don't quote blind. After a free consultation we send a clear, fixed proposal, with milestones and pricing agreed before any work begins.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines follow scope: a focused website or brand identity is measured in weeks, a larger product in months. Your proposal includes a realistic plan, and you'll review working progress at every step.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Yes. We can take over an existing product, extend it, or embed senior engineers alongside your own team — whichever gets you there fastest.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Code, designs and data are yours from day one, documented and ready to hand over whenever you need them.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Launch is where the real feedback starts. Our support plans keep your product healthy and secure, and we keep improving it as you learn what your users need.

We're not technical. Can you still help?

Yes. We explain every decision in plain language, and one accountable lead keeps you up to date without the jargon.

How do we stay in touch during a project?

Through regular check-ins and a shared view of progress. You'll always know what's being worked on, what comes next and what we need from you.
