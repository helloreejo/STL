Before you write.

The questions we're asked most often before a project starts.

How soon will I hear back?

Within one business day, and usually from the person who would lead the work rather than an account manager. If it's urgent, call either office.

What should I put in the brief?

What you're building, roughly when you need it, and what success would look like. Links to anything that already exists help. If you're still working it out, say so — that's a normal place to start.

Do I need a budget in mind?

It helps, even as a range, because it tells us what's realistic. If you'd rather not share one, we'll come back with options at a few different sizes instead.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours with the brief, or ask and we'll send ours over before you share anything sensitive.

Can we talk before I send anything?

Of course. Call either office, or write one line in the form asking for a call and we'll send times that work in your time zone.

Do you work with clients outside Canada and India?

Yes. Between the two offices our working day runs from the Americas through to Asia, so wherever you are there are hours we share.

I need help with something already built.

Tell us what's wrong and what it's built with. If we can help we'll say how; if someone else would be a better fit, we'll say that too.
